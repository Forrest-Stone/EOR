An e-commerce company plans to advertise its new product on three different social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram). The advertising cost on each platform is $1000 per day. According to the company's market research, it is expected to reach potential customers of 2000, 1500, and 1000 people per day on Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram respectively. To maximize the effectiveness of the advertising campaign, the company wants to reach at least 10000 potential customers within 7 days through advertising on these platforms. However, due to budget constraints, the company can only advertise a maximum of 10 times. Assuming that each advertising campaign can reach the expected number of potential customers, and the number of advertising campaigns on each platform must be an integer, the question is: How should the company allocate its advertising campaigns to minimize the total cost while satisfying all the constraints?
