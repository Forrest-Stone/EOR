A port management company needs to arrange three types of cargo ships for transportation: large cargo ships (Type A), medium cargo ships (Type B), and small cargo ships (Type C). Each type of ship has different operating costs and cargo capacities. The company needs to determine how many of each type of ship to operate in order to meet the demand of transporting at least 20,000 tons of cargo. Specifically: One Type A ship can carry 1,000 tons of cargo; One Type B ship can carry 500 tons of cargo; One Type C ship can carry 200 tons of cargo. Due to port berth and maintenance constraints, the port can accommodate a maximum of 60 ships per day (including all types). The cost of operating each type of ship is as follows: Operating one Type A ship costs $30,000; Operating one Type B ship costs $15,000; Operating one Type C ship costs $7,000.Additionally, there are constraints on the maximum number of each type of ship that can be operated: A maximum of 20 Type A ships; A maximum of 30 Type B ships; A maximum of 40 Type C ships. Due to the indivisibility of the ships, the number of each type must be an integer. Under these conditions, how should the company allocate the number of each type of ship to meet the transportation demand while minimizing the total cost? Please round the answer to the nearest integer.
