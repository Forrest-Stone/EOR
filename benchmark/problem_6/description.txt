A city is planning to introduce a new renewable energy source into its energy system to reduce environmental pollution. The city already has two sources of energy: coal-fired power and natural gas. The new renewable energy comes from wind power generation. Each energy source has its specific capacity and unit cost, as shown in the table below: | Energy Source\Parameter | Capacity (MW) | Unit Cost (CNY/MW) | |------------------------|---------------|-------------------| | Coal-fired Power | 5000 | 20 | | Natural Gas | 4000 | 25 | | Wind Power | 3000 | 15 | To ensure energy security, the supply of each energy source cannot exceed its maximum capacity. In addition, for environmental considerations, the supply of wind power should account for at least 20% of the total supply. Assuming the total energy demand of the city is 8000 MW, how can the supply of each energy source be determined to minimize the total cost while meeting the energy demand and environmental requirements?
