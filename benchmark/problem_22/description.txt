A city plans to improve its air quality by implementing four measures (A, B, C, and D). Each measure has different implementation costs and improvement effects. The table below provides the implementation costs and improvement effects for each measure: | Measure | Implementation Cost (in million yuan) | Improvement Effect (reduction in PM2.5 concentration) | |---------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------| | A | 20 | 30 | | B | 30 | 40 | | C | 50 | 60 | | D | 60 | 70 | To ensure health and environmental protection, the city has set the following two goals: 1. The PM2.5 concentration must be reduced by at least 100 units. 2. The cost of implementing any measure must not exceed the city's budget limit (assumed to be 100 million yuan). Which measures should the city choose and implement to achieve the goal of improving air quality while keeping costs within the budget range? Design an implementation plan and calculate the minimum implementation cost.
