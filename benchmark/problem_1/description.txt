A manufacturing company has two production lines (A and B) and needs to complete three production tasks (1, 2, 3). The minimum and maximum energy efficiency consumption standards, energy consumption cost per unit, production cost per unit, production quantity, and total production capacity for each production task and production line are shown in the table below: | Production Task | Minimum Energy Efficiency Consumption (kw) | Maximum Energy Efficiency Consumption (kw) | Energy Consumption Cost per Unit (yuan/kw) | Production Cost per Unit (yuan/piece) | Production Quantity (pieces) | |-----------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------| | 1 | 10 | 20 | 5 | 30 | 300 | | 2 | 15 | 25 | 4 | 35 | 200 | | 3 | 12 | 22 | 6 | 32 | 250 | | Production Line | Total Production Capacity (pieces) | |-----------------|------------------------------------| | A | 400 | | B | 350 | The goal is to minimize total energy consumption and production cost while satisfying the following conditions: - The energy usage for each production task is within the minimum and maximum energy efficiency consumption standards. - The task allocation for each production line does not exceed its total production capacity. The problem is: How should these two production lines be scheduled to complete the three production tasks, in order to minimize total energy consumption and production cost while satisfying the above conditions?
