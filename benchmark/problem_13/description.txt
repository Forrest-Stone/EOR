An internet company operates three data centers, labeled A, B, and C. Each data center has a different number of servers and energy consumption. The company needs to decide which data centers' servers to shut down in order to meet a data storage requirement of at least 10PB. Specifically, one server can provide 0.1PB of storage space. Additionally, the number of servers in each data center cannot fall below their minimum operational requirement to ensure the data centers' normal operation. Furthermore, due to power supply and cooling requirements, the number of servers in data centers A, B, and C cannot exceed 500, 400, and 300, respectively. Data center A consumes 2kW of energy per server, with a minimum operational requirement of 100 servers. Data center B consumes 3kW of energy per server, with a minimum operational requirement of 50 servers. Data center C consumes 4kW of energy per server, with a minimum operational requirement of 30 servers. The company's goal is to minimize the total energy consumption while meeting the data storage requirement. Under these conditions, what is the minimum total energy consumption, and what are the server quantities for each data center?
