A textile factory is seeking to improve the water recovery rates of four different production processes: x1, x2, x3, and x4. These processes may involve dyeing, bleaching, printing, and refining. The goal is to maximize the total water recovery rate of these processes. The allocations must adhere to the following constraints: - The total water consumption of x1 and x2 cannot exceed 5000 liters. - The total water consumption of x2 and x3 must be at least 3000 liters. - The difference in water consumption between x3 and x4 cannot exceed 1000 liters. - The difference in water consumption between x4 and x1 must be at least 500 liters. Consider specific limits for each process (x1: 0-5000 liters, x2: 0-4000 liters, x3: 0-6000 liters, x4: 0-7000 liters) due to technical requirements, environmental regulations, and other factors. The unit water recovery rates for each process are shown in the table below: | Process | Unit Water Recovery Rate | |---------|-------------------------| | x1 | 0.8 | | x2 | 0.7 | | x3 | 0.9 | | x4 | 0.6 | What is the optimal allocation of water resources for these four processes to maximize the overall water recovery rate while satisfying the given constraints? Note that the water allocation for each process must be an integer.
