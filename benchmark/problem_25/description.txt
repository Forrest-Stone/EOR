A healthcare company produces two types of drugs: A and B. Each drug requires two raw materials: Raw Material 1 and Raw Material 2. Each unit of drug A requires 3 units of Raw Material 1 and 2 units of Raw Material 2, while each unit of drug B requires 2 units of Raw Material 1 and 5 units of Raw Material 2. The company has 180 units of Raw Material 1 and 200 units of Raw Material 2 in storage. The production cost per unit of drug A and B is $20 and $30, respectively, while their selling prices are $50 and $70, respectively. To meet market demand, the company must produce at least 30 units of drug A and 20 units of drug B. Additionally, due to production capacity limitations, the company can produce a maximum of 60 units of drug A and 40 units of drug B. The company's goal is to determine the production quantities of each drug in order to maximize total profit (selling revenue minus production cost). What is the best allocation of raw materials to maximize total profit?
