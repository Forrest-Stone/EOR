An environmental organization plans to allocate resources to four different projects: x1, x2, x3, and x4. These projects may involve forest conservation, water resource protection, climate change, and wildlife conservation. The goal is to maximize the total investment in environmental projects while satisfying the following constraints set due to budget limitations and project priorities: - The sum of resources allocated to projects x1 and x2 cannot exceed $10 million. - The sum of resources allocated to projects x2 and x3 must be at least $8 million. - The difference in resources allocated to projects x3 and x4 cannot exceed $2 million. - The resources allocated to project x4 must be at least $3 million more than those allocated to project x1. Additionally, each project has a specific resource limit (x1: 0-5 million, x2: 0-6 million, x3: 0-7 million, x4: 0-8 million) due to budget or operational constraints. Considering that all values are integers, as we cannot have fractional resource amounts, what is the maximum total amount in dollars that the organization can invest in the four projects given an optimal resource allocation within the specified ranges?
