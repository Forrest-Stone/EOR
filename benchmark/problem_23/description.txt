A steel mill has four different production lines: x1, x2, x3, and x4. The operating costs for each line are $50, $100, $200, and $300 per hour, respectively. The objective is to minimize the total cost associated with these production lines. The operation of the production lines must comply with the following constraints: - The total operating time of x1 and x2 cannot exceed 500 hours. - The total operating time of x2 and x3 must be at least 300 hours. - The difference in operating time between x3 and x4 cannot exceed 100 hours. - The difference in operating time between x4 and x1 must be at least 50 hours. Taking into account the maximum operating time limits for each production line (x1: 0-700 hours, x2: 0-600 hours, x3: 0-800 hours, x4: 0-900 hours) due to equipment wear and maintenance requirements, and since all values are integers, as we cannot have fractional operating times, what is the minimum total cost for the company given an optimal allocation of operating times for the four production lines within the specified ranges? Please round the answer to the nearest dollar.
