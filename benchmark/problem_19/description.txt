A logistics company needs to distribute goods between four areas (A, B, C, and D) within a city. The company has two types of vehicles: Type X and Type Y. Type X vehicles have a capacity of 10 tons and an operating cost of $20 per hour. Type Y vehicles have a capacity of 20 tons and an operating cost of $30 per hour. The company needs to determine the number of hours each type of vehicle should operate in order to meet a minimum demand of 100 tons of goods. Specifically, one ton of goods can be transported by a Type X vehicle in 1 hour or a Type Y vehicle in 0.5 hours. However, due to vehicle availability, the sum of the operating hours for Type X and Type Y vehicles cannot exceed 24 hours. Under these conditions, what is the minimum total cost that satisfies the demand for goods while adhering to the operating time limit? Please round the answer to the nearest integer.
