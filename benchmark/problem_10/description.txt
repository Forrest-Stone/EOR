A city is planning to build new parks in four different areas (A, B, C, and D). Each area has different construction costs and expected benefits. The construction costs are as follows: A: 1 million, B: 1.5 million, C: 2 million, D: 2.5 million. The expected benefits are as follows: A: 0.7 million, B: 1.2 million, C: 1.6 million, D: 2 million. At the same time, due to urban planning and environmental requirements, the difference in the number of parks between any two areas cannot exceed 1. In other words, the difference between the number of parks in each area and its adjacent areas cannot be greater than 1. The problem is: How should the parks be allocated to maximize the expected benefits while considering the costs and urban planning requirements, under the given conditions?
