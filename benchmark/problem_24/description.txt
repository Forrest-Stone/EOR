A cold chain logistics company is providing cold chain transportation services for a pharmaceutical company. They have two distribution centers (A, B) and need to deliver drugs to four cities (1, 2, 3, 4). The transportation costs from each distribution center to each city and the transportation capacity of each distribution center are shown in the table below:\n\n| Distribution Center\\City | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Transportation Capacity |\n|-------------------------|---|---|---|---|-----------------------|\n| A                       | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 100                   |\n| B                       | 3 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 200                   |\n\nThe drug demands for each city are as follows: City 1 requires 50 units, City 2 requires 80 units, City 3 requires 70 units, and City 4 requires 100 units.\n\nThe company's goal is to meet the drug demands for all cities while minimizing the total transportation cost.\n\nHow should the cold chain transportation network be optimized to achieve this goal? Please provide the quantity of drugs that each distribution center should transport to each city.
