A bank plans to allocate available funds to two types of loans: personal loans and corporate loans. Each loan type has a specific risk and return rate. The risk of a personal loan is 10% and the return rate is 5%. The risk of a corporate loan is 20% and the return rate is 15%. The bank's risk tolerance limits the risk exposure of personal loans and corporate loans, i.e., the risk exposure of personal loans should not exceed $8,000 and the risk exposure of corporate loans should not exceed $10,000. In addition, the bank wants the total risk exposure of the loan portfolio to not exceed $15,000, and at least half of the funds should be allocated to personal loans. The bank's objective is to maximize the expected return of the loan portfolio while satisfying these constraints. Assuming the bank has $100,000 of available funds, how should it allocate these funds to achieve its objective? Note: Amounts should be rounded to the nearest dollar.
