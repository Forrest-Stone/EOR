A farmer needs to allocate workers on his farm to complete three tasks: wheat harvesting (x1), cow milking (x2), and chicken feeding (x3). Each task requires a certain number of workers, and the wage cost for each worker is a fixed $10 per day. The farmer has the following constraints: 1. The farmer has a maximum of 100 workers available for allocation. 2. The number of workers for wheat harvesting should be at least 30% of the total number of workers on the farm. 3. The number of workers for cow milking should be at most 40% of the total number of workers on the farm. 4. The number of workers for chicken feeding should be at least 20. Given these constraints, how should the farmer allocate workers among these three tasks to maximize the efficiency of task completion (i.e., the number of workers completing the tasks) and minimize wage costs?
