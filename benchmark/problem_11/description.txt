An online education platform is planning how to best allocate its resources to four different learner communities: x1, x2, x3, and x4. These communities may involve different subjects or topics such as programming, mathematics, language learning, and science. The goal of this platform is to minimize the total cost associated with these communities while satisfying certain constraints on community activities, where the unit cost for each community is $50 for x1, $100 for x2, $200 for x3, and $300 for x4. The community activities must adhere to the following constraints: - The total number of activities for communities x1 and x2 cannot exceed 500. - The total number of activities for communities x2 and x3 must be at least 300. - The difference in activities between communities x3 and x4 cannot exceed 100. - The difference in activities between communities x4 and x1 must be at least 50. Considering that each community has specific limits on activities (x1: 0-500, x2: 0-400, x3: 0-600, x4: 0-700), which may be due to factors such as community size or others, and given that all values must be integers (as we cannot have fractional activities), what is the minimum total cost in dollars for the optimal allocation of activities among the four communities, subject to the specified ranges? Please round the answer to the nearest dollar.
