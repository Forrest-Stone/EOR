A fitness enthusiast wants to plan his diet to meet his nutritional needs. His goal is to consume at least 60 grams of protein, 100 grams of carbohydrates, and 30 grams of fat per day. He typically eats three types of food: chicken breast, brown rice, and avocado. The nutritional composition and prices per 100 grams of each food are as follows: | Food\Parameter | Protein (g) | Carbohydrates (g) | Fat (g) | Price (yuan) | |----------------|-------------|------------------|---------|--------------| | Chicken Breast | 30 | 0 | 3 | 15 | | Brown Rice | 3 | 23 | 1 | 10 | | Avocado | 2 | 9 | 15 | 20 | How should he plan his daily food intake to meet his nutritional needs at the lowest price? The intake of each type of food needs to be an integer, as he cannot consume a fraction of a food.
