A university's career guidance center wants to provide personalized career guidance and job recommendations to students based on their personal information and career interests. The center can choose to provide three types of services: S1, S2, and S3. The costs, expected effects, and service times for each type of service are shown in the table below: | Service Type | Cost (yuan) | Expected Effect (score) | Service Time (minutes) | |--------------|-------------|------------------------|------------------------| | S1 | 50 | 70 | 30 | | S2 | 80 | 90 | 50 | | S3 | 100 | 95 | 70 | The center has a daily budget of 500 yuan to provide services for students. Due to time constraints, the center can only provide 8 hours of service time each day. In addition, to ensure the quality and diversity of services, the center requires that each type of service is provided no more than 10 times. Your task is to determine how many times each type of service should be provided to maximize the expected effect while meeting the budget and time constraints, as well as the maximum number of times each service can be provided.
